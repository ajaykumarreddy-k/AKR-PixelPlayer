Frequently Asked Questions

What is AKR Pixel Player?

AKR Pixel Player is an open-source high-fidelity audio player for Android and Wear OS. It combines Google's Media3 ExoPlayer framework with custom C/C++ FFmpeg decoders for uncompressed 32-bit / 384kHz sound rendering.

Does AKR Pixel Player support gapless bit-perfect playback?

Yes! The player uses a native audio pipeline that bypasses default Android sample rate conversion to deliver bit-perfect, zero-latency gapless transitions.

Can I sync playlists with Wear OS smartwatches?

Yes, AKR Pixel Player features standalone Wear OS audio routing. You can transfer offline playlists directly to your watch.
